Campus lost & found — product brief

Lost it?
Someone found it.

CampusFind turns a scattered WhatsApp group and a security-office drawer into a proper system: post it, get matched automatically, prove it's yours, hand it back — without either side ever seeing a phone number.

01

How a return actually happens

Post

Lost or Found, with category, date, campus zone, optional exact pin, photos.

Match

Rule-based scoring surfaces likely counterparts by category, date, zone, colour, brand.

Prove it

Finder holds private proof questions only the true owner can answer.

Hand back

Both sides confirm the physical handover; the listing closes as Returned.

03

What we decided, and why it sticks

AreaDecision
AuthenticationPasswordless email OTP — confirms the inbox, not an institution
EligibilityAny OTP-verified email, registered-user community
Contact privacyEmail & phone stay hidden — always; talk via comments or in-app chat
Claim verificationPrivate proof questions, set and judged by the finder
Public locationApproximate zone shown publicly; exact pin stays restricted
HandoverTwo-party confirmation — no one-sided "returned" claims
College sign-upSearch an approved college, or request a new one (student request → admin approval)
Independent usersNo college? Post anyway — visibility to others is scoped, not blocked
PlatformsNext.js website first, Expo mobile app once the backend is proven
04

Who sees what

Same college

Always visible to each other — that's the core community.

College → independents

Visible by default; each student can opt out in settings.

Independent → college

Only if that college has opted in to being publicly discoverable.

College ↔ college

Closed for now — each campus is its own board.
